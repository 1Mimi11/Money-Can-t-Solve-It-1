// README.md//
Money Can't Solve It is a text-based fishing game, designed to educate players on sustainability and ethical issues in the fishing industry. The player makes choices that lead their fishery -- either as a business that follows regulations and takes into the account of their fishing environment, or one that chases profits over everything.

To play Money Can't Solve It, input commands that correspond to the options presented. For example, to check your stats, enter '3' in the main menu. Every option will have it's command number next to it in brackets [#]. Some commands will require you to type out words; typable command words will look like 'this', surrounded by apostrophies.

I created this game to educate others on sustainable practices and ethical issues within the fishing industry. Many people think that increasing funding for conservation efforts is all it will take to balance the health of our aquatic environments and our aquatic animal demand -- but many can't solve it, our first steps are education. This game is a fun and interactive way to learn about our fishing industry deliemma, play it to learn more about the problems!
